## Design assessment

Keep the beige/navy/orange identity. It is coherent and appropriate to GV operations. The biggest opportunity is continuity: preserve work, explain prerequisites, keep legal-entity/document context visible and provide clear next actions. Do not replace the whole interface with another generic dashboard.

Strengths: explicit entity boundaries; progressive disclosure in capture forms; drafts distinguished from ledger entries; journal review and close checks; exact quote-version acceptance. Cognitive load is moderate overall, high in accountant forms, Team and large purchasing workflows. Eleven purchasing destinations and interdependent access controls need progressive disclosure, not arbitrary removal of accounting modules.
